Frequently asked questions

Owner questions, answered.

Is LuxePoint Suites a traditional tenant?

Yes. LuxePoint Suites leases properties directly and remains responsible for rental obligations, communication and maintaining the property throughout the tenancy.

What happens if a guest damages the property?

LuxePoint Suites remains responsible for the tenancy relationship and maintains operational processes intended to reduce risk and address issues promptly.

Will there be lots of people coming and going?

Guest stays are managed through minimum-stay policies, booking processes and clear house rules designed for responsible accommodation.

What if my building does not allow short stays?

We review building, strata and other applicable requirements before entering into a lease.
